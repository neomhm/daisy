README

Bigger is not Better.

A kinder Kind of AI.

Distributed Artificial Intelligence Serving You

statuscoming soon languages10 small modelson your CPU organizercloud or local

Hand over a folder of your business's own files: invoices, quotes, price lists, spreadsheets, brochures, letters. D.AI.SY turns it into your website, checked at every step. You approve everything before anything is published.

After launch, ask questions about your own data, like "which services earned most last quarter?", and get exact answers in about a second.

Why small models

Most AI today is one giant model that tries to do everything. D.AI.SY does the opposite. Small, fast models, each built for one precise job, do the work on your own computer's ordinary processor. No graphics card needed.

One larger model is the organizer. It's called once per business to write the plan, and it runs on our server or on your own PC. Your choice.

How it works

one-business.ymlfrom your files to your website
  1. ReadEvery file becomes text, in any of ten languages.
  2. UnderstandSmall models mark the facts in your documents, and code checks each one against its source.
  3. PlanThe organizer writes the plan for your site, once. It sees only codes and counts, never your names, addresses or figures.
  4. BuildYour site is assembled from vetted, hand-written building blocks. No model writes code.
  5. CheckCode checks every page: every number matches its source, and nothing is unsourced.
  6. ApproveYou see the finished site and approve, edit or remove every fact, sentence and photo.
  7. PublishOnly what you approved goes live. The last ten versions are kept, so you can always switch back.
  8. AskAfter launch, ask Daisy about your own data. You get the exact answer and the rows it came from.

Principles

What a kinder Kind of AI means to us:

  • Small models, on your own computer. The precise work is done by small, fast models on an ordinary PC.
  • The organizer runs where you choose: on our server, or on your own PC.
  • Code checks everything. No model judges another model, and nothing a model writes is ever run as code.
  • Every value has a source: a line in your documents, a cell in your spreadsheet, or your own answer.
  • "No" is a normal answer. When a model isn't sure, it says so, and the question comes to you instead of a guess.
  • Private stays private. Revenue, staff numbers and client names are never published. Bank and card numbers, ID numbers and salaries are never even extracted.
  • You approve every public fact before anything is published.
