Tulip

Tulip 1.1 · in training

Spreadsheet importer

Hand Tulip a spreadsheet and she reads its preview, then writes a short TulipScript program that imports every row into the right one of your business tables, or refuses the sheet. That program never runs as raw code: a locked-down interpreter checks every instruction. If a sheet doesn't fit, Tulip refuses cleanly. No guessing, no silent damage.

Still in training. Her size is expected, not yet read from the model file, and her score comes from an in-training check on a small sample. Her final tests are larger and harder than 1.0's.

Jasmine

Jasmine 0 · rehearsal model

Design engine

The lightest petal. Jasmine reads your business's features and proposes a complete design spec in milliseconds, right on your CPU: palette, font pair, type scale, corners, density and the order of every section. A grammar lets her write only what can pass the checks, and code verifies every choice before you see it. Jasmine 0 learned from design rules written as code; she will learn taste from the designs owners approve.

Daisy

sql7c · next version in training

Answers questions about your own data

Ask in plain English, like "which services earned most last quarter?". Daisy turns your question into a precise query spec in about 0.3 seconds on a single CPU core. Code checks the spec, compiles it to SQL for SQLite, PostgreSQL or MySQL in 0.4 ms and runs it read-only, then shows you the exact rows and the SQL behind the answer. No GPU needed, and when she doesn't know, she says so.
